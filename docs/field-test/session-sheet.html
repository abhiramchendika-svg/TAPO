TAPO field test · session script

About 30 minutes · full plan in docs/FIELD_TEST.md

Never hold their phone, never look at their statement, never write a card number. Code people F1, F2… not by name.

1 · Consent 2 min · read aloud, then tick on the record

Thanks for helping. We're testing TAPO, a student project that suggests which of your cards to use when you pay. It's a prototype: it doesn't make payments, and it never asks for your card number, CVV, PIN, OTP or bank login. Anything you type stays on your phone; we don't see it. We'll note your answers and timings under a code, not your name. You can skip any part or stop at any time. Is it OK if we quote something you say, without your name? Shall we start?

2 · Without TAPO 3 min

Ask them to name each card they use with a letter and a few words ("A: supermarket card"); write these on the record. Read each scenario, start the timer, stop it when they name a card. Don't react. Then ask: "How sure are you: not sure, fairly sure, or certain?"

S1"You're paying ₹1,200 for groceries at a supermarket. Which of your cards do you use?"
S2"You're filling up ₹2,500 of fuel at a petrol pump. Which card?"
S3"You're paying ₹4,000 for online shopping. Which card?"

3 · Set up TAPO on their phone about 10 min · time it from opening the link to the last card saved

  1. Open tapo-seven.vercel.app/app → Create account: any name and email, and a password they'll remember (there's no reset).
  2. Skip the introduction, then Settings → Remove all cards → Yes, remove to clear the sample cards.
  3. For each card, tap + at the top of Wallet.Nickname = the letter and description. Issuer is a sample list (any choice is fine). Last four digits = any four digits; they're only a label. Enter the base rate, plus category rates and caps, checked on the bank's site if they can. Note checked or estimated.

From here to the end of step 5, note where they get stuck: a pause of more than about 10 seconds, a question, or a wrong screen. Write the screen and what happened. Only help after 30 seconds or if they ask, and count each time you do.

4 · With TAPO 3 min

Read S1–S3 again in the same order, with the app open on Wallet each time. They tap the category under Paying now? and name the card (if TAPO says another card earns more from some amount up, they go by the scenario's amount). Time from the end of your sentence to them naming the card. Then, for scoring, they open Best, tap each category and type its amount: write the top card as TAPO's top card (for a tie, write "A/B").

5 · Last month's missed rewards optional, 5 min · only with a CSV statement on their phone

Insights → Import your statement → pick the card the statement is for → choose the file → import. On Insights, choose Last Month and ask them to read out "You could have earned about ₹… more by switching cards." Write down the figure and the month.

6 · Questions 5 min · write down their words; don't explain or defend TAPO

  1. What, if anything, surprised you?
  2. What was the hardest part of setting it up?
  3. Think of the last time you paid by card. Would TAPO have changed the card you used? Why?
  4. Would you keep TAPO on your phone after today? (Yes, maybe or no.) Why?
  5. What would make you stop using it?
  6. Some similar apps charge a monthly fee. If TAPO did, what's the most you'd pay, if anything?

Ask if you can message them in a week. If they'd rather not keep TAPO, show them Settings → Delete account.

7 · Seven days later send word for word

Hi, thanks again for trying TAPO last week. Two quick questions: in the last 7 days, how many times did you open Best before paying (0, 1–2, or 3 or more)? And did it change which card you used? An example would help.

Type each record into docs/field-test/results.csv the same day. Leave an answer blank rather than filling it in later from memory.
