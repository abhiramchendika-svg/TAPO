# Market note: how people choose a card today

Most people with several cards pick one by habit or memory, and it costs them rewards. The apps that do pick a
card either need your linked accounts, charge a subscription, need a high credit score, or are built for US
banks. TAPO's opening is an answer at the moment of paying, worked out on the phone, with no linked accounts.

## How big the problem is

| Fact | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credit cards in use in India | 12.4 crore, August 2026 | RBI data, reported by [Angel One][angelone] |
| Spent on them in one month | ₹2.02 lakh crore, August 2026 | Same |
| Active cards, and the people holding them | 10.7 crore cards held by 5.2 crore people, March 2026 | TransUnion CIBIL, reported by [Business Today][bt-cibil] |
| Cardholders with 3 or more cards | 22%, up from 12% | Same |
| People whose cards don't match how they spend | 92%, in a study of 129 people | 1 Finance Magazine, reported by [Business Today][bt-1finance] |
| Rewards missed by people spending ₹5–8 lakh a year | ₹38,000–42,000 a year | Same |
| Our interviewees who couldn't name their best card for all of groceries, fuel and online shopping | 7 of 8 | [`INTERVIEWS.md`](INTERVIEWS.md) |

The RBI figures are the latest we confirmed. Check for a newer month before presenting.

## How people solve it today

| Alternative | How it works | What it costs them | Why they'd switch to TAPO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Habit:** one usual card, or whichever is easiest to reach | No effort; seen with P3 and P8 in our interviews | Missed rewards: the 92% and ₹38,000–42,000 figures above | An answer that comes as fast as reaching for the usual card: one tap |
| **Memory:** remembering each card's categories and offers | The person keeps track themselves | It slips: P1 often forgets, P5 doesn't track categories, P7 missed an offer | TAPO holds every card's rates and caps and does the maths, including how much cap is left |
| [**CheQ Wisor**][wisor] (India, 2025) | AI chat that recommends a card per purchase, using data from cards you link | Linking your cards and sharing your transactions | No linked accounts; everything stays on the phone |
| [**PointsFly**][pointsfly] (India, launched August 2026) | Best card per purchase, points tracking, travel booked on points | A paid subscription | Same core job; TAPO focuses on the moment of paying and keeps data on the phone. Our price isn't decided |
| [**CRED**][cred] (India) | Card bills, several cards, credit score, tap to pay | Membership needs a credit score of 750 or more | Open to anyone; its listing doesn't describe picking a card per purchase |
| [**CardPointers**][cardpointers], [**MaxRewards**][maxrewards] (US) | Best card per purchase, plus bank offers | Built around US banks such as Amex, Chase and Citi | Works in rupees with any card you add. They show the demand is real |
| [**Google Wallet**][gwallet] (India, May 2024) | Passes, tickets and loyalty cards; Google Pay stays its payments app in India | Doesn't recommend a card | Not a direct alternative today |

Competitor details come from their own listings and press coverage, not from our testing.

## Why people would switch, and what's still unproven

- **For:** 6 of our 8 interviewees said they'd use TAPO, and 1 said maybe. The less someone knew about their cards,
  the more they wanted it. These are stated intentions, not behaviour.
- **Unproven:** whether people keep using it after a week, how much they actually miss in rupees, and what they'd
  pay. The [field test](FIELD_TEST.md) measures all three.
- **Risk:** CheQ Wisor and PointsFly already do the core job. If they add on-device data or a one-tap answer, our
  difference narrows. Today our edge is privacy and speed at the counter, not features.
