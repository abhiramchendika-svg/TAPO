FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is TAPO a bank or a payment app?

No. TAPO doesn't issue cards, hold money or process payments. It's a guide that tells you which of your existing cards to use. You keep paying with your cards as usual.

Which banks and cards does TAPO support?

Any credit or debit card. You enter each card's reward rates yourself, so TAPO works with any issuer or network. TAPO isn't affiliated with any bank or card network.

Will TAPO ask for my card number or OTP?

Never. TAPO only stores a nickname and the last four digits as a label. It has no way to accept a full card number, CVV, PIN or OTP.

Where is my statement data stored?

On your phone only. Statements are read on your device; TAPO keeps the date, merchant and amount of each purchase and ignores everything else. Long numbers such as account or UPI references are masked.

How accurate are the reward estimates?

They follow the rates and monthly caps you enter, rounded down to the paisa so TAPO never over-promises. Your issuer's statement is always the final word.
