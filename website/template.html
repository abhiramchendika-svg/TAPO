Real spending, not sample data

See what it cost you.

Export a bank or card statement as CSV and TAPO reads it on your device. It finds the date, merchant and amount columns, skips money coming in, sorts each purchase into a category and works out how much more your best card would have earned. This page uses the same parser as the app.

How it works

Three steps to the right card.

STEP 1

Add your cards

A nickname, the last four digits as a label, and the reward rules: a base rate plus category rates and monthly caps.

STEP 2

Import your spending

Load a statement CSV. TAPO reads it on the device, categorises each purchase and ignores money coming in.

STEP 3

Pay with the best card

Ask before you pay, or review afterwards: missed rewards, per-card earnings and the swaps that would earn more.

Safety by design

What TAPO can't leak, it never stores.

Security here is structural. The data model has no fields for sensitive card data, so there is nothing to protect, encrypt or lose.

Never asked for. Never stored.

Full card numberCVVPINOTPExpiry dateBank loginPayment tokenUPI PIN

Nicknames that look like card numbers are rejected. Stored data is re-checked every time the app starts, and anything unexpected is dropped.

  • On the device onlyNo server, no account, no analytics, no tracking. Data lives in the phone's local storage.
  • Statements stay privateParsed locally. Only date, merchant and amount are kept; long numbers are masked to the last four digits.
  • No fake paymentsThe NFC demo is labelled as a simulation on every screen. No payment provider, card network or provisioning code exists.
  • No borrowed credibilityIssuers are fictional, rules are illustrative, and TAPO claims no partnership with any bank or network.
